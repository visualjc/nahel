Question:
When provenance facts overlap, should one row show one primary category or multiple provenance badges, and what precedence should apply?

Rationale:
The human chose additive facts rather than one lossy primary category. Every row keeps the exact resolution-event actor as resolver identity. Badge predicates are deterministic and limited to structured evidence: direct-human when the resolution actor is human; delegated when an agent resolution cites a human-attributed event; ratified when a strictly later human logged note names the ticket; agent when an agent resolution has no cited-human delegation; incomplete independently when required joins fail. Ratified and incomplete may therefore coexist with the resolver-derived badge rather than replacing it. Cross-agent-grilled is intentionally absent: research ticket fvb6ceqv found no typed field that distinguishes a cross-agent grill from ordinary multi-agent research, so actor counts or prose claims cannot truthfully prove that badge. It may be added only after a typed proof marker exists. This agent-attributed resolution relays the human decision without claiming a human actor.
