Ticket ceremony scales down as altitude goes up: PRD level = full map discipline; roadmap level = node mutations are the record plus one journaled session note; ideation level = blessed ideas graduate to later-horizon nodes, rejects land as out-of-scope lines.

Decided by resolving grilling ticket cphjdnrp, charting: Wayfinder-style planning works at three altitudes with granularity to match: (1) roadmap shaping — 5k ft, create/edit/move roadmap nodes like a mind map without going deep; (2) ideation — 10k ft, 'wouldn't it be cool if' PO/CEO-level brainstorm of new features; (3) feature definition — PRD-level, how a feature works and its sub-features. Each altitude gets the partner posture: grills the human for decisions only they can make, runs research/prototype tickets AFK and returns with findings, makes delegated judgment calls, and hands off to build the moment the human says 'enough to start' — no full de-fogging required.

Question:
What does a planning session produce at each altitude — does ticket ceremony scale with granularity?

Rationale:
A roadmap is allowed to change its mind cheaply; forcing ticket-grade rationale on every 'add it to later' would make the 5k-ft view as heavy as the PRD view and kill the mind-map feel the human asked for. The product node's map holds only durable open questions that survive a session. The thinner rationale trail at altitude is accepted deliberately: the session note is searchable via recall, and anything genuinely contested earns a real ticket.
