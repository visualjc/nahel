Ticket type is the delegation line and governance mode moves it: research/prototype/task tickets the partner always resolves itself; grilling tickets wait for the human under 'human' governance and may be self-resolved under 'delegated' or 'agent' — plus a per-ticket human-only flag no mode overrides.

Decided by resolving grilling ticket 4pvfrdzy, charting: Wayfinder-style planning works at three altitudes with granularity to match: (1) roadmap shaping — 5k ft, create/edit/move roadmap nodes like a mind map without going deep; (2) ideation — 10k ft, 'wouldn't it be cool if' PO/CEO-level brainstorm of new features; (3) feature definition — PRD-level, how a feature works and its sub-features. Each altitude gets the partner posture: grills the human for decisions only they can make, runs research/prototype tickets AFK and returns with findings, makes delegated judgment calls, and hands off to build the moment the human says 'enough to start' — no full de-fogging required.

Rationale:
