The planning-partner workflow (nahel/workflows/plan.md) instructs that under human governance the partner must not answer its own grilling questions except through a recorded delegation. The current CLI enforcement only blocks resolve/close/clear on tickets explicitly flagged [human-only]; it does not generally prevent an agent actor from resolving a non-human-only grilling ticket under human governance. Suggest a follow-up to either (a) extend CLI refusals to cover all grilling tickets under human governance unless a delegation source is cited, or (b) make the rule machine-checkable through validation of resolution sources.
