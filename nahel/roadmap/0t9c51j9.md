Every verb that takes --since accepts the same window language: a relative span (7d, 24h) or an ISO-8601 UTC timestamp. Today 'nahel standup' and 'nahel decisions' route --since through resolveSince (src/views/standup.ts) and take both forms, while 'nahel progress' validates it against the raw journal ts schema and rejects 'nahel progress --since 7d' — even though the top-level help advertises '--since 7d|24h|ISO' one line above for standup. Route progress through the same resolver, state the accepted forms in its usage line and error text, and keep any future --since consumer on the shared resolver so the surface cannot drift again.
